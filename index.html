BEFORE YOU BUILD

How to play BuildA

Match, dodge, answer and upgrade.

🍬

1. Match three tiles

Tap one tile, then an adjacent tile to swap them. Match three or more of the same icon to collect materials.

💣

2. Bombs trigger quizzes

Match a bomb tile to pause the board and answer a building-construction question. Every bomb match triggers a question.

🏡

3. Build the 3D house

Correct answers earn a power-up tile and help finish the house. A wrong answer costs 1 second. Complete 3 questions to unlock the next level.
